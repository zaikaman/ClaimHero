## 3. Technology Stack & Architectural Pillars

The hackathon is anchored around an integrated stack designed for autonomous, full-stack applications:

1. **Backend Engine (Convex):**
   - Must serve as the core persistence and compute layer.
   - Utilizes reactive queries, ACID transactions, mutations, background actions, scheduling / crons, file storage, full-text / vector indexing, and HTTP routing.
   - Hosting target: Convex static hosting (`convex.site`).

2. **Data Ingestion & Intelligence (Firecrawl):**
   - Used for live data extraction, web crawling, scraping unstructured web sources, and feeding clean structured data into Convex.

3. **Autonomous Communications & Inboxes (AgentMail):**
   - Provides dedicated programmatic email inboxes for agents.
   - Handles two-way external email exchanges, payer/service transmission, and inbound webhook processing.

4. **Agentic Coding & Intelligence (OpenAI / Codex / Agent Ecosystem):**
   - Participants build using AI coding agents (such as Codex, Antigravity IDE, Claude, or ChatGPT) augmented with Convex agent plugins.
   - Integrates OpenAI models for reasoning, multimodal extraction, and structured synthesis.

## 5. Qualification & Judging Criteria

The hackathon evaluation is scored across 4 official criteria:

1. **Real-world apps, not developer tools:**
   - "Everyday" means a normal user-facing application for a concrete audience and workflow, not necessarily an app used every day or understood by everyone. A focused B2B, B2G, healthcare, legal, or other specialist product is valid if its intended users can use it in a real workflow this week. Developer tools, agent infrastructure, and apps whose primary users are software developers score lower under this criterion.
   - Judges score what you ship on Convex, OpenAI, Firecrawl, and AgentMail. Your hackathon build log is what judges read, so include what you built, the target user and problem, the stack, the live URL, and your demo link.

2. **Creativity and usefulness:**
   - Build something a real person would use this week: law, hospitality, health, construction, whatever you know. Copycats and developer-only tools score low.

3. **Convex depth:**
   - Real use of queries, mutations, live updates, auth, and components. A thin frontend on a hosted page does not count.

4. **Sponsor stack:**
   - OpenAI, Firecrawl, and AgentMail do real work in your product. They generate, crawl, or send, not just sit in the README.

## 7. Eligibility & Compliance Rules

- **Age Requirement:** Participants must be at least 18 years of age.
- **Exclusions:** Employees of Convex, OpenAI, Firecrawl, AgentMail, and co-hosts (including their immediate family members) are ineligible for monetary prizes.
- **Geographic Restrictions:** Open globally, except where restricted or prohibited by United States export controls or local regulations (including Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, and Syria).
- **Intellectual Property:** Submissions must consist of original work and must not infringe upon third-party intellectual property or privacy rights.
